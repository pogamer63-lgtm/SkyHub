## Skills

**Source:** https://hypixel-skyblock.fandom.com/wiki/Skills  
**Fetched via:** Fandom API (direct page returned 403)

### Overview
"There are twelve Skills in Hypixel SkyBlock, which are automatically leveled up based on the actions performed by a player."

### The Twelve Skills

**Combat**
- Maximum Level: 60
- Rewards per level: Crit Chance bonus, increased mob damage
- Methods: Defeating mobs (Zombies, Skeletons, Spiders, Endermen, Ghasts), Combat Minions, Bestiary milestones, Arachne's Burrow

**Farming**
- Maximum Level: 60
- Rewards: HP bonus, Farming Fortune bonus
- Methods: Collecting crops (Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Pumpkins, Melons), Farming Minions, Shearing Sheep

**Fishing**
- Maximum Level: 50
- Rewards: HP bonus, fishing treasures unlock
- Methods: Fishing with rods, killing Sea Creatures, Fishing Minions

**Mining**
- Maximum Level: 60
- Rewards: Defense bonus, Mining Fortune bonus, Reforging unlock
- Methods: Mining ores and stones, Snow Minions, Mining Minions, Commissions

**Foraging**
- Maximum Level: 54
- Rewards: Strength bonus, Foraging Fortune bonus
- Methods: Chopping trees, picking flowers, Foraging Minions

**Enchanting**
- Maximum Level: 60
- Rewards: Intelligence bonus, Ability Damage bonus, increased XP orbs
- Methods: Anvil combinations, Enchanting Table, Experimentation Table

**Alchemy**
- Maximum Level: 50
- Rewards: Intelligence bonus, increased potion duration
- Methods: Brewing potions, Alchemy Minions

**Carpentry**
- Maximum Level: 50
- Rewards: HP bonus, Furniture Recipe unlock
- Methods: Crafting items

**Taming**
- Maximum Level: 60
- Rewards: Pet Luck bonus, increased Pet XP gain
- Methods: Earning Pet XP

**Hunting**
- Maximum Level: 25
- Rewards: Hunter Fortune bonus, mob charm chance increase
- Methods: Hunting mobs, Fusing Shards, Trap and Salt Hunt Milestones

**Dungeoneering**
- Maximum Level: 50
- Rewards: HP bonus, increased dungeon stats
- Methods: Participating in Dungeons

### Cosmetic Skills

**Runecrafting**
- Maximum Level: 25
- Rewards: Higher level Runes access
- Note: "Runecrafting levels up differently depending on the player's Rank. Unranked players can only reach level 3."
- Rank multipliers range from 1.0x (Default) to 3.0x (MVP++)

**Social**
- Maximum Level: 25
- Rewards: Social game access, coins for Amelia's Shop
- Methods: Unique player visits, island visits

## Slayer

**Source:** https://hypixel-skyblock.fandom.com/wiki/Slayer

### Core System
"Slayers are missions which involve killing mobs of a specific type to spawn a strong boss, then slaying that boss."

### Quest Initiation
- Quests start through Maddox the Slayer at the Tavern (-83, 68, -129)
- Via Maddox Batphone (unlocked at Wolf Slayer LVL 3)
- Via Abiphone
- Vampire Slayer quests use Maddox the Slayer (Rift) at Stillgore Château with Maddox Badphone
- Quests require Coins or Motes; only one can be active per dimension

### Boss Mechanics
- "Once a quest is started, the specified amount of Combat XP must be earned from killing the corresponding mob type."
- Tier 3+ quests spawn mini-bosses with higher XP yields
- Bosses spawn at the last mob's location and must be defeated within a time limit or the quest fails
- **Critical Attack:** "All Slayer Bosses will perform a critical attack if the Player stands in one place for too long."
- **Regeneration:** Bosses rapidly regenerate HP if they haven't hit players recently. Immune to Knockback enchantments.

### Slayer Types and Bosses
- **Zombie:** Revenant Horror (default unlock)
- **Spider:** Tarantula Broodfather (requires Revenant Horror Tier II)
- **Wolf:** Sven Packmaster (requires Tarantula Broodfather Tier II)
- **Enderman:** Voidgloom Seraph (requires Sven Packmaster Tier IV)
- **Vampire:** Riftstalker Bloodfiend (requires Globulate Timecharm)
- **Blaze:** Inferno Demonlord (requires Voidgloom Seraph Tier 3)

### Rewards
- **Stat Boosts:** Permanent bonuses per level
- **Combat Wisdom:** +1 for Tier 1–3 bosses, +2 for Tier IV–V (max +34)
- **Token Bonuses:** +3 tokens from mini-bosses (LVL 6+)
- **Discount:** 4% cheaper slayers (LVL 7+)
- **Level 8 Perks:** Unique abilities per slayer type
- **Bartender Mixins:** Special potion effects
- **SkyBlock XP:** Progression currency
- **Bits:** 2–15 depending on tier

### Auto-Slayer
"Auto-Slayer is a feature unlocked upon reaching LVL 6 in the Zombie, Spider and Wolf Slayers." It automatically begins new quests if sufficient coins are available in purse or bank.

### RNG Meter
"The RNG Meter is unlocked for each individual boss type upon slaying their respective Tier 3 boss." XP fills the meter; when complete, the selected item becomes guaranteed on the next kill.

### Notable Trivia
- Without bonuses, 2000 Tier IV quests needed to reach LVL 9
- Costs 100 million coins per slayer (without failures)
- Combat XP buffs stack additively with Hunter Talisman/Ring

## Dungeons

**Source:** https://hypixel-skyblock.fandom.com/wiki/Dungeons

### Core Definition
"Dungeons are PvE raid activities in which a team of 1 to 5 players work together to defeat mobs within a procedurally generated series of rooms, ending in a boss fight."

### Available Dungeons
- Currently released: The Catacombs and Master Mode
- Two additional dungeons (desert and forest themed) have been hinted at

### Party Requirements
- Standard dungeon entry requires minimum 2 players
- Exception: Bingo profiles and players who completed Floor III and defeated the Professor can claim Saul's Recommendation to run solo

### Core Mechanics

**Room Generation by Size:**
- Tiny: 4x4 rooms
- Small: 5x5 rooms
- Medium: 5x6 or 6x6 rooms

**Special Rooms (Always Generated):**
- Green Room (Starting)
- Purple Room (≥2 Puzzle rooms)
- Yellow Room (Mini-boss)
- Pink Room (Fairy)
- Red Room (Blood)
- Orange Room (Trap, Floor III+ only)
- Brown Rooms (Normal rooms, fill remaining space)

### Floor Structure

**Catacombs:** 7 floors + 1 entrance  
**Master Mode:** 7 floors

### Dungeon Item Mechanics
- Items receive stat buffs based on user's Dungeon level
- Items can be upgraded with Essence (up to 5 stars)
- Each star adds 2–10% buff outside Dungeons, 10–50% inside

**Stats Boosted by Levels & Stars:** HP, DEF, STR, DMG, SPD, CD, INT, SCC, Pristine, Mining Speed, Mining Fortune, Farming Fortune

**Stats Boosted by Stars Only:** MF, TD, CC, BAS, FER, AD

**Unknown Boosting Mechanics:** PL, Foraging Fortune

### Class System

**Five Playable Classes:**

**Healer** — Focuses on keeping team alive through healing abilities and damage buffs.

Key Passives:
- Renew: "healing abilities heal the target for 50% additional HP"
- Healing Aura: "Passively heals all living teammates within a 8 block radius for 1% HP per second"
- Revive: Spawns fairy to revive dead teammates
- Orbies: "15% chance to drop an orb that anyone can pick up"
- Soul Tether: "Clicking on a teammate creates a tether connecting yourself to them"
- Overheal: "Healing a player that is full health will convert the healing into an absorption shield up to 20%"

Dungeon Stone Abilities:
- Healing Circle (2s cooldown): "Launches a healing orb that creates a healing circle where it lands. The healing circle heals friendly players inside for 2% of their HP every second"
- Wish (120s cooldown): "Heals everyone in your group to full health and grants them a shields for 20% of their maximum health"

**Mage** — Uses intelligence and magic damage to disable enemies.

Base Stats: +250 INT

Key Passives:
- Mage Staff: "All your melee weapon attacks become ranged. Those attacks deal 30% of their melee damage"
- Efficient Spells: "All abilities have a 25% shorter cooldown"

Dungeon Stone Abilities:
- Guided Sheep (30s cooldown): "Shoots a Guided Sheep, dealing damage based on your Mage level"
- Thunderstorm (500s cooldown): "Unleash a Thunderstorm, striking enemies in a 10 block radius for 15 seconds"

**Berserk** — Specializes in close-quarter combat with high damage output.

Base Stats: +40% Melee Damage, +30 Walk Speed

Key Passives:
- Bloodlust: "Your next hit after killing a monster deals 20% increased damage"
- Lust For Blood: "Every time you hit an enemy, increases the damage you deal by 15% up to a maximum of 250%"
- Indomitable: "Gain 5% of your STR as DEF"
- Weapon Master: "Your melee weapons hit up to 5 enemies within your strike range in a frontal cone"

Dungeon Stone Abilities:
- Throwing Axe (10s cooldown): "Throw an Axe, dealing the same damage as your highest hit in the last minute"
- Ragnarok (60s cooldown): "Grants 100 BAS, 400 SPD and 1.5x melee damage increase for 15 seconds. Also summons 3 Zombie minions"

**Archer** — Uses ranged weapons for safe distance combat.

Base Stats: +150% Arrow Damage, -25% Melee Damage

Key Passives:
- Doubleshoot: "50% chance to shoot a second arrow"
- Bone Plating: "Reduces your next incoming hit by 150 damage"
- Bouncy Arrows: "0% chance for your arrows to bounce to an additional target"

Dungeon Stone Abilities:
- Explosive Shot (40s cooldown): "Shoots a volley of 3 Arrows that explodes on impact"
- Rapid Fire (100s cooldown): "Shoots 5 Arrows per second for 5 seconds"

**Tank** — Draws enemy attention and protects vulnerable teammates.

Base Stats: +100 HP, +50 DEF

Key Passives:
- Protective Barrier: "Grants 1.2x DEF. Upon falling below 50% HP, gain a 10% health absorption shield"
- Taunt: "Increases the chance for mobs to target you when you are above 25% HP"
- Diversion: "Divert 80% of the damage taken by your teammates within 30 blocks around you"
- Defensive Stance: "Grants immunity to knockback from mobs"

Dungeon Stone Abilities:
- Seismic Wave (15s cooldown): "A Seismic Wave emerges from underneath you and travels in a straight line. Deals 20000 + 10% damage for every 50 DEF"
- Castle of Stone (150s cooldown): "Fortifies your defense, reducing the damage you take by 70% for 20 seconds"

### General Dungeon Mechanics

**Mana System:**
"Every time you hit an enemy, you regain 1% max mana +5. This applies to Melee strikes, Bow hits, Mage Staff ability (for Mage class)"
Does NOT apply to: Item abilities, Class abilities

**Speed Stat Adjustment:**
"Any SPD above 100 is reduced by 1/3"

**Disabled/Modified Enchantments:**
- Dragon Tracer: disabled
- Vampirism: "Healing from Vampirism is reduced 10x"
- Venomous: "Slowing effect from Venomous is reduced"

**Modified Items:**
- End Stone Sword ability: does not work
- Fired arrows: "do not respect invulnerability frames, but deal reduced knockback"

**Restrictions:**
- Cannot warp to private islands
- Cannot ride mounts (Horse Pet, etc.)
- Cannot use /ah, /et, /av, /bz with Cookie Buff
- "Players may only consume or splash one potion during a Dungeon run" (exceptions: Healing, Mana, Stamina potions; God Potions prohibited)

### Death and Revival System
Players become Ghosts instead of dying: "invisible except for a ghost icon that follows them and is visible to other players"

**Ghost Capabilities:**
- Fly freely (not through walls)
- Cannot normally interact with world
- Can kill Fairies to revive themselves
- Can use Ghost Abilities
- Can view Magical Map
- Cannot attack Dungeon Mobs (except via Ghost Abilities)

**Revival Methods:**
- Revive Stone in inventory
- Teammate uses Revive Stone
- Kill Fairy in Fairy Room
- Defeat Wandering Soul in boss fight
- Healer class Revive abilities
- Automatic revival: 15s (Entrance), 45s (Floor I), 100s (Floor II)
- Automatic revival upon entering boss fight

### Completion & Rewards

**Run Completion Triggers:**
- Boss defeated
- All players dead/left
- 60 minutes elapsed without boss defeat

Dungeon completion triggers: "Dungeon Score assignment based on exploration, Secrets found, deaths, puzzles failed, and time taken"

Rewards: "up to six Dungeon Reward Chests, which contain rewards of loot and Essence and can be opened for a cost"

**Rewards Awarded For:**
- Dungeoneering XP
- Items from chests
- SkyBlock XP for new floor completion (used for SkyBlock Levels)

## Magical Power

**Source:** https://hypixel-skyblock.fandom.com/wiki/Magical_Power

### Core Definition
"Magical Power is a mechanic that boosts the Stats of Accessory Powers."

### Primary Mechanic
Each accessory in a player's bag grants Magical Power based on rarity level. The accumulated Magical Power determines an Accessory Power multiplier using this calculation:

**Stats Multiplier = 29.97 × (ln(0.0019 × (Magical Power) + 1))^1.2**

### Accessory Rarity Tiers & Values
| Rarity | Magical Power |
|--------|--------------|
| Common | 3 MP |
| Uncommon | 5 MP |
| Rare | 8 MP |
| Epic | 12 MP |
| Legendary | 16 MP |
| Mythical | 22 MP |
| Special | 3 MP |
| Very Special | 5 MP |

### Additional Rules
- Dungeon-exclusive accessories provide double their Magical Power when used inside dungeons
- Players earn 1 Special XP per Magical Power obtained

### Special Accessories
- Hegemony Artifact: double MP
- Rift Prism: 11 MP when imbued
- Abicases: 1 extra MP per 2 contacts

### Historical Timeline
- Introduced April 15, 2022 in the main version
- Version 0.13 in beta testing

## Garden

**Source:** https://hypixel-skyblock.fandom.com/wiki/Garden (redirects to The Garden)

### Core Mechanics
**Access Requirements:** Players must reach SkyBlock Level 5 to access The Garden, a personal farming island similar to the Private Island.

### Garden Levels
The system features 15 levels (I–XV). "Leveling it gives 10 SkyBlock XP per level" and unlocks visitors, barn skins, crops, and crop upgrades. Players gain Garden XP through milestones and visitor offers.

### Crops Available
At maximum Garden Level XV, 13 crops become accessible:
- Wheat, Carrot, Potato, Pumpkin, Sugar Cane
- Melon Slice, Cactus, Cocoa Beans, Mushroom, Nether Wart
- Sunflower, Moonflower, Wild Rose

Each crop unlocks progressively from Garden Level I through Level XII.

### Hunting Methods

**Forest Hunting:** Uses Abyssal Lasso or better to cast at Forest Attributes. Players wait for stamina depletion until "REEL appears." The Hunter's Suppress attribute boosts stamina depletion.

**Water Hunting:** Requires Basic Fishing Net or superior for Water Attributes. Some creatures are caught instantly while others have depleting health. Pull stat determines damage dealt.

**Combat Hunting:** Needs Worn Huntaxe - Genesis and Small Pocket Black Hole or better. Melee weapons insert into huntaxes to transfer stats. Once mob reaches below 10% HP, Black Holes capture it.

**Salt Hunts:** Active Salts grant small charm chance when killing mobs with Attribute Shards.

**Trap Hunts:** "Placing a Huntrap in a location where mobs have Attribute Shards will eventually capture a Attribute Shard."

## Kuudra

**Source:** https://hypixel-skyblock.fandom.com/wiki/Kuudra

### Core Description
"Overgrown lava Kraken. The more sulphur it consumes the stronger it becomes."

Kuudra is a boss found in Kuudra's Hollow, accessed from Forgotten Skull. Keys unlock reward chests after completion.

### Five Tiers with Requirements

| Tier | Requirement | SkyBlock XP Reward |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Basic | Have Elle take you to Kuudra | 20 SkyBlock XP |
| Hot | Complete main quest + 1,000 faction reputation + beat Basic | 40 SkyBlock XP |
| Burning | 3,000 faction reputation + beat Hot | 60 SkyBlock XP |
| Fiery | 7,000 faction reputation + beat Burning | 80 SkyBlock XP |
| Infernal | 12,000 faction reputation + beat Fiery | 100 SkyBlock XP |

### Boss Fight Phases

**Phase 1 — Crates:** Use Lava Rods to fish supply crates from surrounding lava, bring to platform center. Stand until meter reaches 100% to pickup.

**Phase 2 — Ballista:** Sneak and left-click supply piles to build ballista (primary weapon against Kuudra). Mobs attack during construction, resetting progress.

**Phase 3 — Fuel:** Fish 4 fuel cells using Lava Rod. Kuudra must be stunned before firing (Burning tier+), otherwise minimal damage dealt.

**Phase 4 — Stomach (Burning+ only):** Enter via Human Cannonball or Consumption Zone. Break orbs with pickaxe (5% health reduction each). Magma Bacteria spawn and deal knockback.

**Phase 5 — Lair (Infernal only):** Arena collapses revealing lower section. Dodge tentacles, fire at head until health reaches zero. All deaths are final kills unless Support Route member revives with tokens.
