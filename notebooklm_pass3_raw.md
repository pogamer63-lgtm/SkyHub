## Q1: What is the Hunting skill?

**Hunting** is one of the Main Skills in Hypixel SkyBlock that players level up by performing specific actions to capture **Attribute Shards** from mobs. It was officially added to the SkyBlock Prototype on **June 23, 2025** as part of the Foraging Update Part I.

### What the Hunting Skill Does
Leveling the Hunting skill provides permanent stat boosts and rewards:
- **Hunter Fortune:** Grants +1 per level, which improves hunting efficiency.
- **Charming:** Grants a permanent percentage chance to "charm" mobs you defeat, allowing you to capture their Attribute Shard. This chance starts at 0.04% at level I and increases to **1% at level XXV**.
- **Coins and SkyBlock XP:** Each level rewards a set amount of coins (e.g., 225,000 coins at level XXV) and small amounts of SkyBlock XP.

### Hunting Methods
- **Forest Hunting:** Requires an **Abysmal Lasso** (or better). Players cast the lasso at a mob and wait for its stamina meter to deplete until a "REEL" prompt appears.
- **Water Hunting:** Uses a **Basic Fishing Net** (or better). Some aquatic mobs are caught instantly, while others require their health to be depleted using the **Pull** stat.
- **Combat Hunting:** Requires a **Worn Huntaxe - Genesis** and a **Small Pocket Black Hole**. Players damage a mob to below 10% health and then use the Pocket Black Hole to absorb it.
- **Salt Hunts:** Activating a "Salt" provides a small chance to charm defeated mobs and capture their shards.
- **Trap Hunts:** Placing **Huntraps** in specific locations will eventually capture Attribute Shards passively over time.

### XP Table and Max Level
Hunting XP is determined by the shard's rarity:

| Shard Rarity | Hunting XP Gained |
|---|---|
| COMMON | +75 XP |
| UNCOMMON | +150 XP |
| RARE | +300 XP |
| EPIC | +500 XP |
| LEGENDARY | +1,000 XP |

XP is also awarded for reaching milestones in **David Hunterborough's** Trap and Salt Hunts, as well as specific collection milestones (Lushlilac IV, Tender Wood VIII, and Vinesap VIII). The leveling rewards table currently details rewards up to **Level XXV (25)** — this appears to be the current cap.

### Relation to the Foraging Skill
Hunting is fundamentally linked to the Foraging skill:
- Announced as a major component of the 2025 Foraging update.
- Players can earn Hunting XP through milestones in Foraging-related collections (Lushlilac, Tender Wood, Vinesap).
- Wiki categorizes it under "Foraging."
- Several hunting methods (Forest, Trap Hunting) focus on wooded environments from the Foraging update.

## Q2: What changed in the Foraging skill with the Foraging Update Part I?

The **Foraging Update Part I** was released on **June 23, 2025**.

### Changes to the Foraging Skill
- **Max Foraging level increased from 50 to 54.** These extra levels provide up to **94 Strength and 216 Foraging Fortune** at level 54.
- To unlock levels 51-54, players must:
  - Complete **Fig Log IX** and **Mangrove Log IX** collections (+1 each).
  - Purchase two additional level cap increases from **Agatha's Shop** using **Agatha's Coupons** and **Starlyn Prizes** (unlocked at Agatha's Milestone 4).
- The **Hunting skill** was introduced as a new "Main Skill" fundamentally linked to Foraging.

### New Areas
- **Galatea:** Accessible at **Foraging Level 25**, source of new high-XP logs.
- **Moonglade Marsh:** Massive new region containing sub-locations: **Ancient Ruins**, **Forest Temple**, **Murkwater Outpost**, **Tangleburg**, and **Verdant Summit**.

### New Items and Resources
- **Wood:** Fig Logs (+15 XP), Mangrove Logs (+20 XP), their enchanted forms, plus **Figstone** and **Mangcore** secondary materials.
- **Moonglade Resources:** Vinesap, Tender Wood, Sea Lumies, Lushlilac, Candycomb, Oceandy, Deep Roots.
- **Reforges:** **Moonglade reforge** for axes — provides Foraging Fortune and up to +6 Foraging Wisdom based on rarity.
- **Progression currencies:** Agatha's Coupons, Starlyn Prizes, Lushlilac Bonbons.
- **Hunting Equipment:** Abysmal Lasso, Basic Fishing Net, Worn Huntaxe - Genesis, Small Pocket Black Hole, Salts, Huntraps.
- **New enchantments:** Forest Pledge III-V, Woodsplitter I-V (available on Bazaar).

## Q4: How does Vampire Slayer Tier 5 work?

### Vampire Slayer Tier 5
The sources confirm a **Tier V** boss exists. The **Sangria Dye** is a Rare drop with a 5% chance exclusively from Tier V. The boss is the **Riftstalker Bloodfiend** and it is unique among slayers in having **no minibosses**.

General Bloodfiend boss mechanics: critical attack if player stands still too long (sends them airborne), rapid health regeneration if it hasn't hit a player recently.

### Ping Mechanic
The sources **do not document a "ping mechanic"** specifically for Vampire Slayer. The "ping" likely refers to the critical attack warning (green number + up arrow + specific sound) when a player stands still too long — but it is not labeled as a "ping" in wiki sources.

### RNG Meter
- **Unlocked** for each boss type once you defeat their **Tier III** boss.
- Any XP from Tier III to Tier V bosses fills the meter.
- Players select a specific rare item from the boss's drop table.
- Once the meter is full, that item is **guaranteed to drop** on the next kill.

### Erythrocyte Coins
The sources **do not mention "Erythrocyte coins."** Vampire Slayer uses:
- **Motes** — currency to start quests.
- **Coven Seals and Bloodbadges** — primary crafting resources from the boss.

Erythrocyte coins may be a community-coined term or from an update not covered in these sources.

## Q5: What are Greenhouse mutations?

**Greenhouse mutations** are special crops that have a chance to grow in the Greenhouse when specific standard crops are arranged in particular patterns. They can be identified using a **Crop Analyzer**. Harvesting mutations has a chance to drop **Ethereal Vines**, which are required to unlock additional Greenhouse plots and eventually a second/third Greenhouse.

### New Crop Types

| Crop / Mutation | Base Yield | Growth Cycles | Crop Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sunflower** | 256x Sunflower | 15 | **Bonus Drops:** Extra loot pool rolls |
| **Moonflower** | 256x Moonflower | 15 | **Bonus Drops:** Extra loot pool rolls |
| **Wild Rose** | 256x Wild Rose | 15 | **Effect Spread:** Spreads buffs/debuffs to adjacent crops |

Note: **Soggybud** not mentioned in sources. **Bambloom** appears only as a **Bambloom Shard** in the Bazaar — specific crop mechanics not documented.

### Farming Fortune Relation
Sources do not use the term "Farming Fortune" for these crops directly. Instead they use:
- **Harvest Boost:** +20% crop yield
- **Improved Harvest Boost:** +30% crop yield

Harvesting Greenhouse crops can yield **Harvest Bounty** events, dropping rare items like **Overgrown Grass** — a reforge stone that provides Farming Fortune when applied to equipment.

## Q6: What are the HOTM Tier 10 nodes?

Reaching **HotM Tier 10** requires **1,247,000 HotM XP** cumulative and unlocks 7 specialized nodes.

### Pickaxe Abilities (Active — choose one at a time, 120s cooldown)
- **Gemstone Infusion:** Increases effectiveness of every Gemstone in drill slots by **100%** for 20–30 seconds based on level.
- **Sheer Force:** Grants **200 Mining Spread** for 20–30 seconds based on level.

### Passive Perks
- **Mining Master:** +0.1 to +1.1 Pristine (at max level including Blue Cheese bonus).
- **Crystalline:** +0.5% to +25.5% chance of finding a Glacite Mineshaft containing a Gemstone Crystal.
- **Gifts from the Departed:** 0.2% to 20.2% chance to receive an extra item when looting a Frozen Corpse.
- **Dead Man's Chest:** 1% to 51% chance to spawn one additional Frozen Corpse upon entering a Glacite Mineshaft.
- **Vanguard Seeker:** 1% to 51% chance to find a rare Vanguard Corpse within Glacite Mineshafts.

### Additional T10 Rewards
- **Core of the Mountain Level 10:** Requires Tier 10, rewards +2 Tokens of the Mountain.
- **Daily Grind and Daily Powder:** At Tier 10, each grants +5,000 of the respective area's powder.

## Q7: What is the current recommended slayer progression order?

The order is dictated by **unlock requirements**:

1. **Zombie Slayer (Revenant Horror)** — No requirements; default starting slayer.
2. **Spider Slayer (Tarantula Broodfather)** — Requires defeating a Tier II Revenant Horror.
3. **Wolf Slayer (Sven Packmaster)** — Requires defeating a Tier II Tarantula Broodfather.
4. **Enderman Slayer (Voidgloom Seraph)** — Requires defeating a Tier IV Sven Packmaster.
5. **Blaze Slayer (Inferno Demonlord)** — Requires defeating a Tier III Voidgloom Seraph.
6. **Vampire Slayer (Riftstalker Bloodfiend)** — Separate unlock: requires securing the **Globulate Timecharm** in the Rift Dimension.

**Note:** The order from wiki is Zombie -> Spider -> Wolf -> Enderman -> Blaze, NOT Zombie -> Wolf -> Spider as previously noted in some community guides. Spider unlocks before Wolf.

### Why This Order
- Permanent stat buffs per slayer: Zombie = Health, Spider = Crit Damage, Wolf = Speed, Enderman = Intelligence.
- Reaching **Level 6** in Zombie/Spider/Wolf unlocks **Auto-Slayer**.
- Reaching **Level 7** in Zombie/Wolf/Spider makes all quests **4% cheaper**.
- Reaching **Level 8** unlocks unique **Bartender Mixins** (Zombie Brain, Wolf Fur, etc.).
- High-tier items (e.g., Terminator bow) require specific slayer levels.

## Q8: What changed with Swappable Pet Items in patch 0.24.1?

Patch 0.24.1 was released on **January 27, 2026** and introduced a major rework to pet items: players can now **retrieve** them instead of permanently losing them on replacement.

### Retrieval Mechanic
Applying a new pet item now **refunds the previous pet item** with a coin fee based on the rarity of the item being applied:

| Rarity | Cost to Swap/Retrieve |
|---|---|
| COMMON | 25,000 coins |
| UNCOMMON | 50,000 coins |
| RARE | 100,000 coins |
| EPIC | 250,000 coins |
| LEGENDARY | 500,000 coins |
| MYTHIC | 1,000,000 coins |
| DIVINE | 2,500,000 coins |

**Super Scrubber** (from Plumber Joe): Removes a pet item without equipping a new one, but the rarity-based fee still applies and Super Scrubber is consumed.

### Item Rarity Adjustments in 0.24.1
- **All Skills Exp Super-Boost:** COMMON -> UNCOMMON
- **Brown Bandana:** RARE -> EPIC
- **Dwarf Turtle Shelmet:** RARE -> EPIC
- **Quick Claw:** EPIC -> LEGENDARY
- **Washed-up Souvenir:** LEGENDARY -> EPIC
