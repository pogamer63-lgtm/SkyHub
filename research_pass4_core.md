## Q5: Explain the dungeon class system in detail: all 5 classes, what each class does, stat bonuses per class level, and the current meta recommendation for each game stage.

In Hypixel SkyBlock, the Dungeon class system allows players to choose from five distinct roles, each providing unique passive abilities, active Dungeon Stone abilities, and ghost abilities used after death [1-3].

### **The Five Dungeon Classes**

#### **1. Healer**
The Healer focuses on team sustainability and survivability [1].
*   **Role:** Uses healing tools and abilities to keep the team alive while contributing to damage [1].
*   **Key Passives:** **Renew** (heals targets for 50% more), **Healing Aura** (passive healing within 8 blocks), and **Revive** (spawns a fairy to revive teammates) [4].
*   **Dungeon Stone Abilities:** **Healing Circle** (area-of-effect healing) and **Wish** (heals everyone to full health and grants a shield) [5].
*   **Stat Bonuses per Level:**
    *   +1% Renew Healing [6].
    *   +2 Ghost Healing Potion Health [6].
    *   +0.4% Wish Shield Percent [6].
    *   -1s Revive Cooldown [6].
    *   +0.4% Overheal Maximum Shield [6].
    *   **Every 2 levels:** +1% Ghost Healing Aura [6].
    *   **Every 5 levels:** +0.1% Healing Aura, +0.5% Circle Healing, +0.5 Circle Radius, and +1 Circle Duration [6, 7].

#### **2. Mage**
The Mage is the primary magic damage dealer, relying on high Intelligence [1].
*   **Role:** Uses magical weapons and the **Mage Staff** passive to shut down enemies from a distance [1, 7].
*   **Key Passives:** **Mage Staff** (converts melee attacks to ranged, dealing 30% damage plus a bonus scaled by Intelligence) and **Efficient Spells** (25% shorter cooldowns) [7].
*   **Dungeon Stone Abilities:** **Guided Sheep** (homing damage) and **Thunderstorm** (high area damage) [8].
*   **Stat Bonuses per Level:**
    *   +5 Intelligence [9].
    *   Increases Guided Sheep, Fireball, and Thunderstorm damage [9].
    *   **Every 2 levels:** +1% Cooldown Reduction, +40% Melee Damage, and +30 Walk Speed [9].

#### **3. Berserk**
The Berserk specializes in high-intensity, close-quarters combat [1].
*   **Role:** Relies on life steal and high melee damage to sustain themselves while clearing mobs [1].
*   **Key Passives:** **Bloodlust** (damage boost after kills), **Lust for Blood** (increased damage per hit on a single enemy), and **Weapon Master** (melee hits strike up to 5 enemies in a cone) [9].
*   **Dungeon Stone Abilities:** **Throwing Axe** (ranged melee damage) and **Ragnarok** (massive speed/attack speed boost and minions) [10].
*   **Stat Bonuses per Level:**
    *   +0.4% Melee Damage and +0.4% Bloodlust Damage [11].
    *   +1% Throwing Axe Damage [11].
    *   +0.6% Lust for Blood damage increase per hit [11].
    *   +0.1% Indomitable (Strength to Defense conversion) [11].
    *   +0.1 Weapon Master Swing Range [11].
    *   **Every 5 levels:** +0.4 Walk Speed, +2 Ghost Potion Strength, and +70% Lust for Blood cap [11, 12].

#### **4. Archer**
The Archer is a high-DPS ranged class specializing in bow combat [1].
*   **Role:** Takes down high-health enemies from a safe distance using bows and shortbows [1].
*   **Key Passives:** **Doubleshoot** (50% chance for a second arrow) and **Bouncy Arrows** (arrows can bounce to additional targets) [12].
*   **Dungeon Stone Abilities:** **Explosive Shot** (area damage) and **Rapid Fire** (high-speed arrow volley) [12].
*   **Stat Bonuses per Level:**
    *   +1% Extra Arrow Chance [13].
    *   +1.6% Arrow Damage [13].
    *   +2 Bouncy Arrow Chance [13].
    *   **Every 5 levels:** -2s Explosive Arrow Cooldown [14].
    *   **Every 10 levels:** +1s Rapid Fire Duration [14].

#### **5. Tank**
The Tank is the team's defensive anchor [1].
*   **Role:** Draws enemy attention and absorbs 80% of the damage taken by teammates within 30 blocks [1, 14].
*   **Key Passives:** **Protective Barrier** (1.2x Defense), **Taunt** (mobs target you), and **Defensive Stance** (knockback immunity) [14].
*   **Dungeon Stone Abilities:** **Seismic Wave** (line damage based on Defense) and **Castle of Stone** (70% damage reduction and massive aggro) [15].
*   **Stat Bonuses per Level:**
    *   +1 Defense and +0.1% Total Defense [16].
    *   +1000-20000 Seismic Wave Damage [16].
    *   +10 Absorption Health [16].

## Q7: How does Magical Power scaling work? What is the formula or scaling curve? List all available Power options and describe what each one does.

**Magical Power (MP)** is a gameplay mechanic that determines the strength of a player's **Accessory Power** [1]. By collecting accessories and placing them in the Accessory Bag, players gain MP, which then acts as a multiplier for the stats provided by their selected Power [1].

### Magical Power Scaling and Formula
Magical Power uses a logarithmic scaling curve, meaning that while your stats increase as you gain more MP, the rate of increase slows down (diminishing returns) at higher levels [1].

The **Stats Multiplier** is calculated using the following formula:
**Stats Multiplier** $= 29.97 \times (\ln(0.0019 \times (\text{Magical Power}) + 1))^{1.2}$ [1].

The final stats granted by a player's chosen Accessory Power are the base stats of that power multiplied by this calculated multiplier [2]. 

### Magical Power per Rarity
The amount of Magical Power an accessory grants is strictly tied to its **rarity**, with the exception of a few specific items [2, 3].

| Accessory Rarity | Magical Power Granted |
| :--- | :--- |
| COMMON | 3 |
| UNCOMMON | 5 |
| RARE | 8 |
| EPIC | 12 |
| LEGENDARY | 16 |
| MYTHIC | 22 |
| SPECIAL | 3 |
| VERY SPECIAL | 5 |

**Special cases and bonuses include:**
*   **Dungeon Accessories:** These grant **double Magical Power** while the player is inside a Dungeon [1, 3].
*   **Hegemony Artifact:** This specific accessory always grants **double** its normal Magical Power [3].
*   **Rift Prism:** When imbued at Erihann, it grants **11** Magical Power [3].
*   **Abicases:** These grant **1 extra Magical Power** for every two contacts the player has [3].

### Available Power Options
The sources indicate that Accessory Powers are unlocked and managed through **Power Stones** at the **Thaumaturgist** [4]. 

**Note on Source Limitations:** While the sources confirm that different Power options exist and are unlocked via Power Stones, **the provided documents do not contain a specific list of the names (e.g., Sighted, Silky, Bloody) or the individual stat descriptions for these powers.** To obtain the specific stat breakdowns for each named Power, you may need to consult the Thaumaturgist NPC in-game or external resources not included in these sources.

However, the sources do mention that for every point of Magical Power you earn, you receive **1 SkyBlock XP** [2]. Additionally, reaching **1,000 MP** awards the **Magical Sigma** emblem, and reaching **2,000 MP** awards the **Golden Magical Sigma** emblem [5].

When used for star upgrades, each star applies a 2-10% stat boost outside of Dungeons and a 10-50% boost inside [4]. Dungeon items receive a 10% stat boost per star while in the Catacombs, with most items capping at 5 stars, though items upgraded with Crimson Essence can reach 15 stars [2].

## Q10: What are the S+ score requirements for each dungeon floor? How is the dungeon score calculated (completion time, skill bonuses, deaths, etc.)?

The sources provided indicate that a **Dungeon Score** is assigned at the end of each run based on your team's performance, but they do not list the specific numeric point thresholds required for an S+ rank on each floor [1]. However, the sources do highlight that achieving an **S+ rank is required to unlock the Bedrock chest** starting from Floor 5, which contains some of the game's strongest items [2].

### **Dungeon Score Calculation**
The final score is determined by the following five performance categories [1]:

*   **Exploration:** This is based on how much of the dungeon has been explored and cleared [1]. A "Magical Map" tracks room completion: a **white checkmark** appears when all required mobs in a room are slain, and a **green checkmark** appears when all secrets are found [3].
*   **Secrets Found:** Finding each **Dungeon Secret** adds to the score for that run [4]. Secrets also provide loot or team-wide buffs known as **Blessings** [4].
*   **Deaths:** The number of times players die negatively impacts the score [1].
    *   **Spirit Pet Bonus:** If you have a **Spirit Pet**, the score penalty for the first player to die in a dungeon is **reduced to 1 point** [5].
*   **Puzzles:** The number of **puzzles failed** during the run [1].
*   **Time Taken:** The speed at which you clear the dungeon and defeat the boss affects the final score [1].

### **Additional Scoring Factors**
*   **Leaving Penalty:** If you leave a dungeon run without killing the boss, the total score is **reduced by 30%** [2].
*   **Boss Completion:** While you do not need to clear every room or kill every mob to finish a dungeon, the run is considered "finished" once the boss is defeated, everyone has died or left, or **60 minutes** have passed [1].
*   **Class Buffs:** While not directly mentioned as a score "bonus," using a single instance of a class in a party increases that class's buffs by **100% of their base stats**, which can help improve completion time and survivability [6].
