## Q1: Garden System

**Question:** Explain the complete Garden system in Hypixel SkyBlock: how to unlock it, all 20 levels and what they give, the crop upgrade system, visitor rewards, and the pest system.

**Answer:**

To unlock the **Garden** system in Hypixel SkyBlock, you must reach **SkyBlock Level 5**, at which point an NPC named **Sam** will appear on your Private Island to provide access. The Garden is a dedicated farming island where players can build custom plots, use presets, and progress through specific Garden levels that are distinct from the Farming skill.

### Garden Levels and Rewards

While you requested information on 20 levels, the sources specify that **The Garden currently has 15 levels** (Garden I – Garden XV). You begin at Level 1 automatically upon your first visit. Leveling up is achieved by earning **Garden XP** from completing Garden Milestones and accepting Visitor offers.

The progression and major unlocks include:
- **Level 1:** Unlocks **Wheat**
- **Level 2:** Unlocks **Carrots**, Tier I Crop Upgrades, and the **Anita** visitor
- **Level 3:** Unlocks **Potatoes** and the ability to purchase more plots
- **Level 4:** Unlocks **Pumpkins** and Tier II Crop Upgrades
- **Level 5:** Unlocks **Sugar Cane**, the **Pest system**, and rewards including 78 total Visitors and 40% increased crop growth
- **Level 6:** Unlocks **Melon Slices** and Tier III Crop Upgrades
- **Level 7:** Unlocks **Cactus** and the **Greenhouse** (after a short quest)
- **Level 8:** Unlocks **Cocoa Beans** and Tier IV Crop Upgrades
- **Level 9:** Unlocks **Mushrooms**
- **Level 10:** Unlocks **Nether Wart**, Tier V Crop Upgrades, and rewards including 120 total Visitors and 90% crop growth
- **Level 11:** Unlocks **Sunflowers** and **Moonflowers**
- **Level 12:** Unlocks **Wild Roses** and Tier VI Crop Upgrades
- **Level 13–14:** Unlocks Tier VII and VIII Crop Upgrades
- **Level 15:** Unlocks **Tier IX Crop Upgrades**, the **Spaceman** visitor, and rewards including 137 total Visitors and 140% crop growth

### Crop Upgrade System

**Crop Upgrades** are managed at the **Desk** in the Barn and are purchased using **Copper**. Each upgrade tier provides a permanent **+5 Farming Fortune** for that specific crop.
- **Tier I to IX:** Costs range from **5 Copper** at Tier I to **4,000 Copper** at Tier IX
- Higher tiers require higher Garden levels, with Tier IX requiring **Garden Level 15**

### Visitor Rewards

NPCs will periodically visit your Garden to offer trades, requesting specific amounts of crops or their derivatives. Serving them provides the following rewards:
- **Standard Rewards:** Farming XP, Garden XP, Copper, and 5 Bits (affected by Fame rank)
- **Garden XP Scaling:** The amount of Garden XP scales with both your Garden Level and the rarity of the Visitor (Uncommon, Rare, Legendary, Mythic, or Special)
- **Rare Drops:** Every visitor has a chance to drop a **Flowering Bouquet (2%)**, **Overgrown Grass (0.1%)**, or a **Green Bandana (0.4%)**
- **Unique Items:** Certain visitors give unique items on their first visit, such as **Tia the Fairy** and **Bruuh**, who both grant a **Fairy Soul**

### Pest System

The Pest system is unlocked at **Garden Level 5**.
- **Spawning:** One of 13 unique pests (such as Flies, Rats, or Mosquitoes) can spawn while you are farming or every hour the Garden is unloaded
- **Negative Impact:** If **4 or more pests** are present, your **Farming Fortune is reduced**; this penalty increases up to a maximum of 8 spawned pests
- **Hunting:** Pests must be killed using **Vacuums**
- **Rewards:** Slaying pests grants **+1,500 Farming XP** (+6,000 for Field Mice), crop drops, and unique items
- **Currency:** Pests drop a special currency that can be traded with **Pesthunter Phillip** for items like **Pest Repellent** or temporary Farming Fortune

## Q2: Farming Fortune

**Question:** What is Farming Fortune? Give the complete formula and list ALL sources of Farming Fortune including gear, enchantments, pets, garden upgrades, Jacob's perks, and Bestiary.

**Answer:**

**Farming Fortune** is a core statistic in Hypixel SkyBlock that **grants extra drops** while harvesting crops. It applies to all "vanilla" crops (wheat, carrots, potatoes, etc.) but does not increase the rate of Farming XP gain, which is based on the number of crop blocks broken rather than the number of items obtained. Additionally, Farming Fortune **increases the chance to obtain rare drops from Pests**.

It is important to note that Farming Fortune is **disabled on your Private Island**; it only functions on the Garden and public farming islands.

### The Farming Fortune Formula

While the sources do not provide a literal mathematical equation, they describe the mechanic as follows: Farming Fortune determines the chance to receive multiple items from a single block. For every **100 points of Farming Fortune**, you gain a **100% chance for an extra drop**. For example, 300 Farming Fortune would guarantee 3 extra drops (totaling 4 items per block).

### Sources of Farming Fortune

#### 1. Farming Levels (Farmhand Perk)
- **Farmhand:** For every Farming level you reach, you gain one level of the Farmhand perk
- **Scaling:** Each level of Farmhand grants **+4 Farming Fortune**
- **Totals:** At Farming 50, you have +200 Fortune; at Farming 60, you have **+240 Farming Fortune**

#### 2. Gear (Armor & Equipment)
- **Melon Armor & Upgrades:** Includes Cropie, Squash, Fermento, and Helianthus Armor sets
- **Farmer & Rancher's Boots:** Both grant **+1 Farming Fortune per Farming level** while you are on the Garden
- **Lantern Helmet:** Grants **+1 Farming Fortune per Farming level** while you are holding an axe
- **Lotus & Blossom Equipment:** Sets like the Blossom Equipment provide various farming bonuses
- **Specific Items:** **Zorro's Cape** and the **Pest Vest** (used in specific set combinations)

#### 3. Reforges and Gemstones
- **Reforges:** Various reforges provide "Frm.Frt" (Farming Fortune), including **Blessed** (for tools), **Bountiful**, **Jaded** (for armor/tools), **Rooted** (for equipment), **Lush**, **Green Thumb**, **Peasant's**, **Robust**, and **Fortunate**
- **Gemstones:** **Peridot Gemstones** can be slotted into compatible gear to increase Farming Fortune

#### 4. Enchantments and Chips
- **Harvesting:** Applied to Hoes
- **Sunder:** Applied to Farming Axes
- **Dedication:** A rare enchantment that can be obtained from the Ravenous Rhino or Librarian visitors
- **Green Thumb:** Applied to equipment; its bonus scales based on the number of unique Garden visitors you have served
- **Cropshot Chip:** A tool-specific upgrade

#### 5. Pets
- **Elephant Pet:** A primary source of Farming Fortune
- **Mooshroom Cow Pet:** Grants a bonus to Farming Fortune based on your **Strength** stat
- **Rose Dragon Pet:** Grants **+3 Farming Fortune per Farming level**
- **Bee Pet:** Provides general Farming Fortune

#### 6. Garden Upgrades
- **Plots:** Every plot you unlock in the Garden grants a permanent **+3 Farming Fortune**. Unlocking all 24 plots provides a total of **+72 Fortune**
- **Crop Upgrades:** Purchased at the Desk using Copper. Each crop has 9 upgrade tiers; each tier grants **+5 Farming Fortune** for that specific crop (up to +45 total)
- **Unique Visitors:** Serving unique visitors indirectly increases your Fortune by powering up the **Green Thumb** enchantment (up to 137 unique visitors)

#### 7. Jacob's Perks & Contests
- While Jacob's Contests are primarily used to **increase the Farming Level Cap** (up to level 60), doing so is the only way to max out the **Farmhand** perk mentioned above

**Note on Bestiary:** The sources mention that mobs killed by minions do not count toward Private Island Bestiary progress, but they **do not list Farming Fortune** as a direct reward for reaching Bestiary milestones. Bestiary typically rewards Health or Wisdom, which is distinct from Farming Fortune.

## Q3: Jacob's Farming Contests

**Question:** Explain Jacob's Farming Contests completely: how they work, medal system, unique golds, and what perks/rewards they give.

**Answer:**

**Jacob's Farming Contests** are competitive events in Hypixel SkyBlock where players compete to harvest the most of a specific crop within a set timeframe. These contests are essential for late-game farming progression, specifically for increasing the Farming level cap and obtaining specialized gear.

### How the Contests Work

Jacob's Farming Contests occur periodically. During a contest, three different crops are selected, and players have **20 minutes** to harvest as much of those specific crops as possible.
- **Participation:** Players can participate from any location where crops grow, though **The Garden** is the most efficient due to custom plot layouts and the fact that Farming Fortune functions there (unlike on Private Islands)
- **NPC Locations:** Jacob and Anita are the primary NPCs for this system. While they originally appeared in the Hub, they have been relocated to a **hut near the Barn** in the Garden
- **Tracking:** Players can view their standing in real-time to see which "bracket" they currently fall into based on their collection compared to other participants

### The Medal System

Rewards are distributed based on your final standing relative to all other players who participated in that specific crop's contest. The rewards consist of **Jacob's Tickets** and **Medals**:
- **Bronze Medal:** Awarded to the top 60% of participants
- **Silver Medal:** Awarded to the top 25% of participants
- **Gold Medal:** Awarded to the top 5% of participants
- **Jacob's Tickets:** A currency awarded alongside medals, used for purchasing items and upgrades from **Anita**

### Unique Golds and the Level Cap

One of the most critical aspects of the contest system is the **Unique Gold** requirement. To maximize the Farming skill, players must achieve "Mastery" in different crop types.
- **Mastery:** You "master" a crop by placing in the **Gold bracket** for that specific crop type
- **Level Cap Increases:** The default Farming level cap is 50. To reach **Farming Level 60**, you must purchase cap upgrades from Anita. Each upgrade requires a **Gold Medal from a unique crop type** that you haven't used for a cap upgrade before
- **Progression Cost:**
  - **Levels 51–55:** Require 1 Gold Medal each (plus 0–100 Tickets)
  - **Levels 56–59:** Require 2 Gold Medals each (plus 150–250 Tickets)
  - **Level 60:** Requires 3 Gold Medals and 500 Jacob's Tickets

### Perks and Rewards

Beyond the level cap, Jacob's Farming Contests provide several significant benefits:

1. **Farmhand Perk Scaling:** Each Farming level gained (up to 60) grants one level of the **Farmhand perk**, which provides **+4 Farming Fortune per level**. Reaching level 60 provides a total of **+240 Farming Fortune** from this perk alone
2. **Anita's Shop:** Medals and Tickets can be traded with Anita for powerful farming items, including:
   - **Mathematical Hoe Blueprints:** Used to craft hoes that scale in power based on the amount of a specific crop you have harvested
   - **Enchantments:** Such as **Delicate** (prevents breaking stems) and **Harvesting**
   - **Rank Upgrades:** Additional permanent Farming Fortune for specific crops
3. **SkyBlock XP:** Reaching Farming Level 60 awards a total of **1,000 SkyBlock XP**
4. **Anita's Personal Pest Perk:** Higher participation and progression in the farming system allow players to access perks that mitigate the impact of Pests in the Garden

## Q4: Fishing System

**Question:** Explain the complete fishing system: sea creatures, trophy fishing, Backwater Bayou, and fishing fortune sources.

**Answer:**

**Fishing** is a core player skill in Hypixel SkyBlock that involves catching items, treasure, and **Sea Creatures** by casting a rod into water or lava. Progressing in the skill grants permanent **Health** bonuses, increased **Treasure Chance**, and the ability to use more advanced gear and encounter tougher mobs.

### Sea Creatures

Sea Creatures are unique mobs that have a chance to spawn instead of an item catch. They drop specialized loot and grant significant Fishing XP when killed.
- **Catching Mechanics:** Your chance to encounter one is determined by your **Sea Creature Chance (SCC)** stat. Additionally, **Double Hook Chance** determines the probability of fishing up two creatures simultaneously.
- **Scaling Difficulty:** As your Fishing level increases, you unlock stronger creatures with better loot. For example, a **Squid** is available at level 1, while the **Lord Jawbus** requires level 45 and "Adept" trophy fisher status.
- **Regional Lists:**
  - **Water (General):** Includes mobs like the Sea Walker, Carrot King, and the Legendary Water Hydra
  - **Crimson Isle (Lava):** Home to powerful mobs like the Magma Slug, Fire Eel, and the Mythic Plhlegblast
  - **Event-Specific:** Certain creatures only appear during the **Spooky Festival** (e.g., Grim Reaper), **Winter Event** (e.g., Yeti), or the **Fishing Festival** (Sharks)
  - **Other Regions:** Specific mobs are found in the **Crystal Hollows** (Water Worms), **Oasis** (Oasis Rabbit), and **Galatea** (The Loch Emperor)

### Trophy Fishing

Trophy Fishing is a specialized system conducted in the lava of the **Crimson Isle**.
- **The System:** Players can catch 18 unique types of **Trophy Fish**, each with specific tiers: **Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond**
- **Requirements:** Most fish have unique catching conditions; for instance, the **Slugfish** requires the bobber to be active for 30 seconds, while the **Golden Fish** must be spotted swimming in the lava
- **Rewards:** Caught fish can be filleted by the NPC **Odger** to receive **Magmafish**. Discovering higher tiers of fish increases your Trophy Fishing level, which unlocks rewards from Odger, better lava fishing rods, and the ability to fish up stronger lava Sea Creatures

### Backwater Bayou

The **Backwater Bayou** is a grimy sewer outflow area featuring unique fishing mechanics and loot.
- **Unique Creatures:** This location features a dedicated set of Sea Creatures including the **Trash Gobbler** (Lvl 5), **Banshee** (Lvl 10), **Alligator** (Lvl 20), and the massive **Titanoboa** (Lvl 30)
- **Junker Joel's Shop:** The NPC Junker Joel sells unique **Rod Parts** (Hooks, Lines, and Sinkers) specifically themed around the Bayou. Notable items include the **Treasure Hook**, which exclusively allows for item/treasure catches, and the **Junk Sinker**, which increases Treasure Chance in the Bayou but replaces all treasures with junk

### Fishing "Fortune" and Stat Sources

While "Farming Fortune" is a single stat, fishing efficiency relies on several distinct stats.

**Sea Creature Chance (SCC):**
- **Armor:** Progression from **Angler** (+4%) to **Shark Scale** (+10%) and **Magma Lord** (+18%)
- **Rods:** Specialized rods like the **Rod of the Sea** (+7%) or the **Hellfire Rod** (+14%) provide major boosts
- **Pets:** The **Dolphin** and **Ammonite** are primary sources

**Fishing Speed:**
- Determines how quickly the bobber bites
- Sources include various rods (up to +180), baits (like **Fish Bait** at +45), and the **Ammonite**, **Dolphin**, or **Flying Fish** pets

**Treasure Chance:**
- Determines the rarity of item catches (Good, Great, or Outstanding)
- Increased by Fishing levels (+0.1 per level), **Blessed Bait**, the **Blessing** enchantment, and a **Rare+ Hermit Crab pet**

**Trophy Fish Chance:**
- Increased by **Hunter Armor** sets (up to +30) and **Odger's Teeth** accessories (up to +4%)

**Blazing Fortune:**
- A specific attribute found on **Lava Flame Shards** that improves lava fishing yields

- **New Island - Galatea:** A high-level foraging and fishing area that requires **Foraging Level 25** to access
- **Level Cap Increase:** The Foraging skill cap was extended from level 50 to **Level 54**

- **Tree Gifts:** Harvesting Fig or Mangrove trees now rewards players with **Tree Gifts**, which are the primary source of **Tender Wood** and **Vinesap**
- **Logger Perk:** Foraging levels now scale the **Logger** ability, providing **+4 Foraging Fortune** per level. At level 54, players have **216 Foraging Fortune** and **94 Strength** from the skill

### The Hunting Skill

**Hunting** is a new **Main Skill** added to the core skill list. It centers around tracking, trapping, and harvesting creatures and specialized wood on Galatea.

#### Max Level

#### XP Sources

- **Scaffolding:** Recipes for **Tiny, Medium, and Large Scaffolding** are unlocked via the Fig Log collection

## Q6: Kuudra

**Question:** Explain Kuudra completely: tiers, requirements, drops, armor sets (Aurora through Molten), and the faction system.

**Answer:**

**Kuudra** is a massive lava kraken boss found in **Kuudra's Hollow**, accessible through the **Forgotten Skull** on the Crimson Isle. The fight is designed for groups of 2 to 4 players and is central to endgame progression for gear and attributes.

### Kuudra Tiers and Requirements

There are **five progressive tiers** of the Kuudra boss fight. Each tier requires the player to have defeated the previous one and meet specific **Faction Reputation** milestones.

| Tier | Reputation Requirement | Quest/Other Requirements | SkyBlock XP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic** | None | Have Elle take you to Kuudra | 20 XP |
| **Hot** | 1,000 Reputation | Complete main quest; beat Basic | 40 XP |
| **Burning** | 3,000 Reputation | Beat Hot | 60 XP |
| **Fiery** | 7,000 Reputation | Beat Fiery | 80 XP |
| **Infernal** | 12,000 Reputation | Beat Fiery | 100 XP |

### The Faction System

Players must align with either the **Mage** or **Barbarian** factions on the Crimson Isle.
- **Reputation:** Earning reputation with your chosen faction is mandatory to unlock higher Kuudra tiers
- **Emissaries:** Each faction has an Emissary (Mage or Barbarian) who sells the **Kuudra Keys** necessary to open paid reward chests after a successful fight
- **NPCs:** Major faction NPCs include Queen Nyx (Mage) and Chief Scorn (Barbarian)

### Armor and Equipment Sets

Kuudra is the primary source for several powerful endgame armor sets and "Molten" equipment. These items are found in **Paid Reward Chests**.

**Armor Sets:** There are five main sets, each catering to different playstyles:
- **Aurora Armor:** Focused on Mage/Intelligence stats
- **Crimson Armor:** Focused on Melee/Combat stats
- **Terror Armor:** Focused on Ranged/Bow stats
- **Fervor Armor:** Focused on Tank/Defense stats
- **Hollow Armor:** A specialized set often used for support

**Molten Equipment:** This set consists of four pieces of equipment (not armor): the **Molten Necklace**, **Molten Bracelet**, **Molten Cloak**, and **Molten Belt**.

### Drops and Loot System

After defeating Kuudra, two chests appear: a **Free Chest** and a **Paid Chest**.
- **Free Chest:** Always contains **Crimson Essence** (amounts scale from ~1-2 in Basic to 200 in Infernal)
- **Paid Chest:** Requires a **Kuudra Key** corresponding to the tier of the fight (e.g., an Infernal Kuudra Key for an Infernal run)
- **Guaranteed Paid Rewards:** Every paid chest is guaranteed to give Crimson Essence, **Kuudra Teeth**, an **Attribute Shard**, and a piece of gear (Armor or Molten equipment)
- **Enchanted Books:** Special books like **Fatal Tempo**, **Inferno**, and various **Mana** enchants (Ferocious, Hardened, Strong, and Vampire) drop exclusively from these chests
- **Attribute Shards:** These are used to add or upgrade stats on Kuudra gear. Examples include **Vitality** (Kraken Shard), **Breeze** (Wither Specter Shard), and **Magic Find** (Cinderbat Shard)
- **Rare Drops:** Higher tiers have chances for extremely rare items like the **Burning Kuudra Core** or **Tentacle Dye**
- **Boss Collection:** Defeating Kuudra progresses a collection that rewards **Kuudra Pets** (Common through Legendary), Kuudra Chunks, and bonus Essence/Teeth

## Q7: The Rift

**Question:** Explain the Rift: how to access it, what content exists, Vampire Slayer location and Motes currency uses.

**Answer:**

The **Rift Dimension** is a unique, time-limited location in Hypixel SkyBlock that operates under its own set of rules, stats, and currency.

### How to Access the Rift

To enter the Rift, you must reach **SkyBlock Level 12**. The entrance is the **Wizard Portal** located in the **Wilderness** near the mountain.

Access requires a **Dimensional Infusion**, which can be obtained in the following ways:
- **Free:** You gain one free infusion every 4 hours, and can store up to 3 at a time
- **XP Bottles:** Pay the Wizard NPC 32x Grand Experience Bottles
- **Bits:** Pay the Wizard 200 Bits (Auto-Buy is also available)
- **Rift Prism:** Having this item in your inventory, accessory bag, or imbued through Erihann provides access

An infusion is only consumed once you leave the Wizard Tower (Rift) or if you join three different Rift instances with the same infusion.

### Existing Content and Locations

The Rift consists of **9 main locations**, each with unique tasks and resources:
- **Wyld Woods**
- **Black Lagoon**
- **West Village**
- **Dreadfarm** (Home to unique crops like Agaricus Caps and Wilted Berberis)
- **Village Plaza**
- **Living Cave** (Where you mine Living Metal Hearts from "snakes")
- **Colosseum**
- **Stillgore Chateau** (The center for Vampire Slayer content)
- **The Mountaintop** (A high-level area containing Timite)

**Key Mechanics:**
- **Rift Time:** Your primary stat. You start with 480 seconds (8 minutes), which depletes while you are in the Rift. When it hits zero, you are teleported back to the Hub
- **Isolated Stats:** Standard stats, gear, and potion effects from the main dimension **do not function** in the Rift. You use a specialized set of stats including Rift Damage, Rift Time, and Hearts
- **Rift Collections:** Includes unique resources like the Half-Eaten Carrot, Hemovibes, and Timite

### Vampire Slayer Location

The **Vampire Slayer** is located within the **Stillgore Chateau**. This area features mobs such as **Thralls** and **Fledglings**, which drop **Hemovibes** used for the Vampire Collection and Slayer progression.

### Motes Currency and Its Uses

**Motes** are the primary currency used within the Rift Dimension.

**How to Obtain:**
- **Orbs:** Walking into floating particles (orbs) found around the Rift rewards 25 Motes and +2 Rift Time
- **Combat:** Killing Rift mobs and completing bosses
- **Selling:** Items can be sold to the **Motes Grubber** NPC for Motes

**Uses:**
- Motes are used to purchase Rift-specific items and progression upgrades from various NPCs within the dimension
- Note that **Rift-Exportable** items (items that can be moved to the main dimension) lose their Mote value once they are transferred through the Rift Transfer Chest

Based on the sources, the "best" money-making methods are defined by their mechanics, rare drop possibilities, and progression requirements rather than live market-tracking "coins per hour" leaderboards. ### Active Money-Making Methods

#### 1. High Investment (Late/End Game)

**Infernal Kuudra:** This is a top-tier combat method requiring **12,000 Faction Reputation** and completion of the previous four tiers.
- **Rewards:** Guaranteed Crimson Essence, Kuudra Teeth, and Attribute Shards
- **Big Hits:** The **Burning Kuudra Core** has a **0.51% drop chance** in the paid chest, and rare **Attribute Shards** (like Magic Find or Vitality) are highly valuable

- **Rewards:** Lord Jawbus has a chance to drop a **Radioactive Vial (0.5%)**, high-value Attribute Shards, and **Bobbin' Scriptures (4%)**

**The Spaceman (Garden):** A special visitor that only appears after reaching **Garden Level 15** and serving 75 unique visitors.
- **Rewards:** Serving him rewards a **Space Helmet**, one of the rarest items in the game, though his request requires items worth approximately **1,000,000,000 coins** to an NPC

#### 2. Medium Investment (Mid Game)

**The Garden (Visitor Hunting):** Serving NPCs in the Garden.
- **Rewards:** Every visitor has a **0.1% chance** to drop **Overgrown Grass** and a **2% chance** for a **Flowering Bouquet**
- **Investment:** Requires Garden Level progression and various crop collections to satisfy requests

**Slayer-Based Fishing:** Hunting specialized creatures like the **Phantom Fisher** or **Grim Reaper** during the Spooky Festival (requires Fishing 21–26).
- **Rewards:** Rare drops like the **Deep Sea Orb (1%)** and **Vampire Fang (10%)**

**Vampire Slayer (Rift):** Accessible at **SkyBlock Level 12** in the Stillgore Chateau.

#### 3. Low Investment (Early Game)

**Water Fishing for Treasures:** Accessible at low fishing levels.
- **Rewards:** Base coin treasures yield **1,000–21,000 coins** in water, which then scales up based on your Fishing level using the formula: MaxCoins = BaseMaxCoins × (1 + FishingLevel / 2.5)

**Flipping (NPC/Bazaar/AH):** The wiki highlights tutorials for **NPC Flipping**, **Bazaar Flipping**, and **AH Flipping** as core introductory methods

### Passive Money-Making (Minions)

Minion setups provide steady income while offline. The investment level is determined by the tier of the minion and the quality of the fuel used.

**Low Investment:** Basic setups like **Clay** or **Snow Minions** are recommended because they are stable and should be maxed out for profit

**Mid-to-High Investment:**
- **Lapis Minions:** Used as an example of high ROI; a Tier XII setup takes approximately **348 days** to pay off its crafting cost before becoming pure profit
- **Slayer Minions:** Revenant and Tarantula minions are highly profitable, but it is generally suggested to stop at **Tier IV** for the best profit-to-cost ratio

**Maximum Efficiency Upgrades:**
- **Fuel:** **Everburning Flames** (+40%) or **Plasma Buckets** (+35%) provide infinite speed boosts
- **Upgrades:** **Flycatchers** (+20% speed) and **Super Compactor 3000s** are essential for long-term passive income
- **Automated Shipping:** Using **Enchanted Hoppers** allows minions to sell their inventory automatically for 90% of the NPC price if they become full

## Q9: Best Reforges

**Question:** What are the best reforges for combat armor, mining armor, farming armor, and accessories?

**Answer:**

Reforging is a mechanic that adds additional stats to gear based on its rarity. The following are the specialized and recommended reforges for different categories of armor and the status of accessories:

### Combat Armor Reforges

Combat reforges vary depending on whether the player prioritizes melee damage, magic damage, or survivability:
- **Ancient:** Added in 2020, this is a top-tier combat reforge for players seeking high damage output
- **Necrotic:** Ideal for Mage builds as it focuses on providing **Intelligence**
- **Loving:** Specifically applied to chestplates to boost ability damage
- **Renowned:** A high-level reforge that provides a percentage boost to most stats
- **Giant:** The primary choice for players focusing on survivability, as it significantly increases **Health**
- **Spiked:** A general combat reforge that provides a balance of offensive stats

### Mining Armor Reforges

Mining-specific reforges are designed to increase **Mining Speed** and **Mining Fortune**:
- **Jaded:** Generally considered the **best reforge for mining armor**, as it provides a substantial bonus to Mining Fortune
- **Prospector's:** A reforge added in 2022 that focuses on mining stats
- **Sturdy:** Provides a boost to Defense and other mining-related utility

### Farming Armor Reforges

Farming reforges are used to maximize crop yields or experience gain:
- **Rooted:** When applied to Blossom Equipment, this reforge grants the **highest amount of Farming Fortune** currently possible
- **Sunny:** This reforge increases **Farming Wisdom**. It is particularly effective on **Bat Person Armor**; when used on The Garden with a Night Saver, the Farming Wisdom bonus is doubled, making it optimal for claiming Visitor rewards
- **Lush, Peasant's, and Robust:** These were added in 2022 as specialized options for farming efficiency
- **Green Thumb:** An equipment reforge whose bonus scales based on the number of unique Garden visitors the player has served

### Accessories

It is important to note that **accessories can no longer be reforged**.
- **Accessory Powers:** Following the Crimson Isle update, the reforge system for accessories was replaced by **Accessory Powers**, which are managed through the Thaumaturgist
- **Legacy Reforges:** Any old reforges remaining on accessories no longer provide any stat bonuses

## Q11: Minion System

**Question:** How does the minion system work? Which minions are best for passive income and why?

**Answer:**

The **minion system** is a core mechanic in Hypixel SkyBlock that allows players to generate resources and earn coins passively while offline. Minions are NPCs placed on a player's Private Island that automatically harvest resources within a **5x5 area**.

### How the Minion System Works

**Mechanics:** Minions operate on a "Time Between Actions" cycle. They perform an action (like placing a block or spawning a mob) and then another action to harvest it. Collectable resources are generated **every other action**; for example, if a minion has a 14-second cooldown, it produces one item every 28 seconds.

**Upgrading:** Minions can be upgraded from **Tier I to Tier XI** (and Tier XII for many types) to increase their speed and storage capacity. Tier XII upgrades are obtained from specialized NPCs like **Bulvar** (Mining), **Tony** (Farming), or **Einary** (Snow/Ice).

**Minion Slots:** Players start with 5 slots and can unlock up to **31 slots** total. Slots are gained by crafting **unique minions** and tiers, or through Profile Upgrades at the Community Shop.

**Interface and Customization:** Each minion has four modification slots:
- **Fuel:** Increases production speed (e.g., **Enchanted Lava Buckets** for a permanent +25% boost or **Plasma Buckets** for +35%)
- **Automated Shipping:** Automatically sells items when the minion is full. The **Enchanted Hopper** is the best option, selling items for 90% of their NPC value
- **Minion Upgrades (x2):** Essential upgrades include the **Super Compactor 3000** (compacts items into enchanted forms) and the **Flycatcher** (+20% speed)
- **Skins:** Purely cosmetic

### Best Minions for Passive Income

Choosing the "best" minion depends on current Bazaar prices and your investment level, but the sources highlight several consistent options:

1. **Snow and Clay Minions (Stable Income):**
   - **Why:** These are recommended for being highly stable and should be **maxed out to Tier XII** for maximum efficiency. Snow minions have extremely fast action speeds, making them excellent when paired with a **Diamond Spreading** upgrade.

2. **Slayer Minions (Revenant and Tarantula):**
   - **Why:** These often provide the highest profit, but it is generally recommended to keep them at **Tier IV**. This is because the high cost of upgrading to Tier XI or XII often results in a very long "pay-off" time before the minion becomes profitable.

3. **Lapis Lazuli Minions:**
   - **Why:** They can be very profitable on the Bazaar, but have a high ROI (Return on Investment) threshold. For example, a **Tier XII Lapis Minion** takes approximately **348 days** to pay off its own crafting cost before it begins generating pure profit.

4. **Minions to Avoid for Coins:**
   - **Lapis and Glowstone** are sometimes not recommended with a **Super Compactor 3000** because their enchanted block forms can be worth significantly less than the raw materials
   - **Fishing and Rabbit minions** are generally not suggested for pure coin-making as they produce many different item types that clog up storage

### Maximizing Efficiency

To get the most out of a passive setup, players should use **Minion Crystals** (like the Farm or Mithril Crystal) to provide a **+10% speed boost** to minions in their radius. Additionally, having a **Level 100 Pet** (like the Rabbit for farming or Ocelot for foraging) equipped while on your island can increase minion speed by **+30%**.

## Q12: Fairy Souls

**Question:** What are all the Fairy Soul exchange bonuses? How many total fairy souls exist?

**Answer:**

There are currently **267 Fairy Souls** in total scattered across the various islands and dimensions of SkyBlock.

### Fairy Soul Exchange Bonuses

Fairy Souls are exchanged in **groups of 5** with the NPC **Tia the Fairy**, who is located in the Wilderness. The bonuses for exchanging them are as follows:

- **SkyBlock XP:** For every group of 5 souls exchanged, you receive **10 SkyBlock XP**
- **Backpack Slots:** You unlock additional **Storage Backpack slots** at specific exchange milestones. These slots are granted at the following exchange numbers: **#1, #2, #4, #6, #8, #10, #13, #16, #19, #22, #25, #28, #31, #34, #37, and #41**

It is important to note that **permanent stat bonuses** (such as Health, Strength, Defense, and Speed) were **removed** from the Fairy Soul system in September 2022 and replaced with the current SkyBlock XP and backpack slot rewards.
